📌 What is MEGAH Staking?

MEGAH Staking is a decentralized staking program for $MEGAH tokens on the BNB Smart Chain. It combines:

  • 💰 Daily ROI — calculated per second
  • 🔗 10-Level Referral System — earn from your network
  • 👑 Leader Bonus — extra rewards for leaders
  • 🛡️ 200% Max Cap — sustainable system protection
  All data is on-chain, transparent, and cannot be manipulated by anyone — including the admin.
📊 The Business Model — How the System Creates Value for Everyone

Why does this system work for everyone — not just Leaders?

1. Token Locking (Supply Shock)
When you stake, your $MEGAH tokens are locked in the smart contract — they cannot be withdrawn. This permanently reduces circulating supply, creating upward pressure on the token price. Everyone who stakes supports the ecosystem and earns rewards.

2. Sustainable Rewards (ROI) — FOR EVERYONE
The daily ROI is funded by the tokenomic reward pool — not by new users' money. Every staker earns this daily, regardless of their network size or Leader status.

3. Network Effect — FOR EVERYONE!
The 10-level referral system creates a powerful network effect. Every single person who stakes and has an active downline earns referral bonuses — no Leader status required!
💡 The more people you refer, the more you earn — and you don't need to be a Leader to benefit.

4. Leadership Incentives — EXTRA for Leaders
Registered Leaders receive an extra bonus from every ROI claim in their network. This is a bonus on top of the referral bonuses that everyone already earns. It encourages experienced users to build and mentor communities — creating a self-growing ecosystem.

📌 Summary — Your Benefits (Without Being a Leader):
✅ Passive Income: Daily ROI without lifting a finger
✅ Network Income: Earn 10-Level Referral Bonuses from your entire referral tree
✅ Token Appreciation: Locked supply supports price growth
✅ Transparent & Secure: All on-chain, no hidden fees
✅ No Leader Status? No problem! You still earn from your network!

👑 Extra Benefits If You Become a Leader:
✅ +Leader Bonus from all network ROI claims
✅ NOT counted against Max Cap — pure extra income
✅ Earn from everyone in your network, not just direct downlines
✅ Recognition as a community leader

  Core Philosophy: Everyone who stakes and builds a network wins. Leaders get extra for their contributions, but everyone benefits from the referral system.

Strategies to reach Max Cap faster:

❓ Frequently Asked Questions — Everything You Need to Know

🔹 Can I earn bonuses from referrals?
✅ Yes! Anyone with active staking who hasn't reached Max Cap earns 10-Level Referral Bonuses from every downline ROI claim.

🔹 Do I need to be a Leader to get referral bonuses?
❌ No! Referral bonuses are for everyone. Leaders only get an extra bonus on top.

🔹 How do I become a Leader?
👑 Leaders are registered by the admin. It's not automatic — it's a reward for active community builders.

🔹 What are the gas fees?
⛽ All on-chain transactions require BNB for gas. Gas costs vary with network congestion.

🔹 What's the minimum stake?
📊 Minimum is read from the contract (subject to admin updates).

🔹 Can I unstake my tokens?
❌ NO. Staked MEGAH is permanently locked. Only rewards can be claimed.

🔹 When can I claim rewards?
⏰ Anytime! As long as you have pending rewards and haven't reached Max Cap.

🔹 What happens at Max Cap?
🛑 You stop earning ROI and referral bonuses. But Leader Bonus continues (exempt from Cap).

🔹 Is my data secure?
🔒 We do not store any personal data. All interactions are on-chain via your wallet.

🔹 Why did my transaction fail?
🔧 Check: MEGAH balance, BNB for gas, token approval, and that you're on BNB Smart Chain.

🔹 What is the Admin Leftover Wallet?
📤 A wallet that receives undistributed referral bonuses when recipients have reached Max Cap.

Based on the $MEGAH tokenomics, 10% of the total MEGAH supply is allocated for campaigns, promotions, and community rewards. One of the programs supported by this allocation is the MEGAH Staking V2 reward program.
