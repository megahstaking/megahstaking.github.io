Referral Levels

The referral rate is stored on-chain in BPS (10,000 BPS = 100%). The interface reads the current values from the contract. The configured 10-level schedule is 10% / 3% / 2% / 1.5% / 1% / 0.75% / 0.6% / 0.5% / 0.4% / 0.25%, equal to 1,900 BPS (19%) when summed as percentages.

Transparency

The most important source of truth is the deployed contract and its current on-chain state. Use the dashboard to monitor your position, use the referral tab to inspect your network, and verify transactions in your wallet or a BNB Smart Chain block explorer. If the interface and the contract state ever differ, treat the on-chain state as the reference.

How to Use MEGAH Staking

User Guide
Simple flow: connect wallet → approve MEGAH → stake → monitor rewards → claim when eligible. Keep BNB in the wallet for blockchain transaction fees.

Step-by-Step

1

Connect Your Wallet

Open the staking page with a Web3 wallet and connect it. Confirm that the wallet is using BNB Smart Chain.

2

Prepare MEGAH & BNB

Hold the amount of $MEGAH you want to stake and keep enough BNB for transaction fees.

3

Set Your Referrer

If you were invited by another user, use the correct referral link/address before staking. The referrer relationship is recorded by the contract when the stake is created.

4

Approve MEGAH

Click Approve MEGAH and confirm the token approval in your wallet. This allows the staking contract to use the approved amount.

5

Stake MEGAH

Enter your amount, review the transaction, then confirm Stake MEGAH. Wait for the blockchain transaction to confirm.

6

Monitor Your Position

Dashboard data shows your staked amount, reward progress, pending rewards, cap status, and other contract-derived information.

7

Claim ROI

When ROI is available, use Claim ROI. The wallet will ask you to confirm the contract transaction and pay the required BNB gas fee.

8

Claim Referral Bonus

If you have eligible referral rewards, use Claim Referral. Referral rewards are based on the network and contract rules, not simply on the number of wallets in your network.

How the 10-Level Referral Works

Your network is counted by relationship depth: L1 is your direct referral, L2 is a referral of your L1, and so on through L10. The current configured rates are: L1 10%, L2 3%, L3 2%, L4 1.5%, L5 1%, L6 0.75%, L7 0.6%, L8 0.5%, L9 0.4%, L10 0.25%. Referral earning is tied to the contract's reward events and eligibility rules.

Good Practice

  • Check the contract address before approving or staking.
  • Read the transaction details in your wallet before signing.
  • Keep enough BNB for future claims and other transactions.
  • Use the Referral tab to inspect L1–L10 instead of relying only on the headline totals.
  • Verify important transactions on-chain and do your own research (DYOR).
Can I withdraw my original staked MEGAH?

According to the current interface documentation and contract rules, staked MEGAH is permanently locked. The claim functions are for eligible rewards; they are not an unstake function.

Does having a downline automatically mean referral income?

No. A wallet being in your network does not by itself guarantee a reward. Referral earnings depend on the contract's reward event and eligibility conditions.

Where should I check the real values?

Use the dashboard and Referral tab for a readable view, then verify important values and transactions against the deployed smart contract and the BNB Smart Chain explorer.
