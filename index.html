📌 What is Megah Staking?

Megah Staking is a decentralized staking program for $MEGAH tokens on BNB Smart Chain.

  • 💰 Daily ROI — calculated per second
  • 🔗 10-Level Referral System
  • 👑 Leader Bonus
  • 🛡️ 200% Max Cap
💰 How Much Can I Earn?

Daily ROI: (Staked Amount × DailyROI_BPS) / 10000

Referral Bonus: Earn from all 10 levels when downlines claim ROI

Leader Bonus: Extra bonus for registered leaders (exempt from Cap)

🔗 How Does Referral Work?

✅ EVERYONE with active staking earns 10-Level Referral Bonuses from their referral network!

✅ NO special status required — it's automatic!

Requirements: Active staking, not reached Max Cap, your downline claims ROI.

🛡️ What is 200% Max Cap?

Max Cap = Staked Amount × 2

  • Example: Stake 100 $MEGAH → Max Cap = 200 $MEGAH
  • Once reached, no further ROI or referral bonuses
  • Leader Bonus continues (exempt from Cap)
❓ FAQ

Can I earn referral bonuses? ✅ Yes! Anyone with active staking earns 10-Level Referral Bonuses.

Do I need to be a Leader? ❌ No! Referral bonuses are for everyone.

Can I unstake? ❌ NO. Staked MEGAH is permanently locked.

When can I claim? ⏰ Anytime! As long as you have pending rewards.
