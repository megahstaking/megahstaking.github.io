How Staking Works

01 — Connect Wallet Connect an EVM-compatible wallet and make sure it is using the network supported by this staking pool.
02 — Approve Token If required, approve the staking contract to use the amount of supported tokens you want to stake.
03 — Stake Tokens Enter your staking amount, confirm the transaction in your wallet, and wait for the transaction to be confirmed on-chain.
What is the minimum stake?

The minimum stake is 1 MEGAH.

You can stake any amount above this minimum. There is no maximum limit, but your reward cap will be 200% of your total staked amount.

How do Sponsor Bonus Levels work?

Sponsor Bonus Levels is a 15-level matching bonus system. You earn commissions from people in your referral network up to 15 generations deep.

How it works:

  • Level 1: People you directly refer (your downline)
  • Level 2: People referred by your Level 1 downline
  • Level 3-15: Continuing down the chain

When do you earn?
Every time your downline claims their ROI, a percentage of their claimed amount is distributed as sponsor bonus to their upline (you) based on the level.

Example:

  • Your Level 1 downline claims 100 MEGAH ROI
  • Level 1 BPS = 500 (5%) → You earn 5 MEGAH
  • Your Level 2 downline claims 100 MEGAH ROI
  • Level 2 BPS = 300 (3%) → You earn 3 MEGAH

Requirements to receive bonuses:

  • ✅ You must have an active stake (totalAmount > 0)
  • ✅ You must not have reached your Maximum Cap

You do NOT need to refer 15 people directly. You can earn from just 1 direct referral (Level 1), and if that person refers others, you also earn from them (Level 2-15).

💡 Tip: Check your Sponsor Bonus Pending in the Dashboard to see how much bonus is waiting to be claimed.

Is this actually staking?
Yes. You deposit the supported token into the staking pool, and your staking position is recorded on the blockchain. Any rewards and limits shown in the DApp are based on the current rules and state of the deployed staking pool.
How does staking work?
Connect your wallet, enter the amount you want to stake, approve the token when required, and confirm the staking transaction. After the transaction is confirmed, your position is read from the blockchain and displayed in the dashboard.
What is Maximum Cap?
Maximum Cap is the reward limit associated with your staking position according to the current staking pool rules. The DApp reads your Maximum Cap from your on-chain staking record rather than calculating a separate value in the interface.
What is the difference between staking rewards and Sponsor Bonus?
Staking rewards are generated from your own staking position. A Sponsor Bonus is a separate reward that can come from the pool's sponsor or referral system. When available, the DApp shows these balances separately so you can see what is available for each type of claim.
How long can I receive staking rewards?
Rewards are not presented as an unlimited promise. Your available staking reward is determined by the deployed contract and your current on-chain position. Reward accumulation stops when the applicable limit for that position has been reached.
How do I know how much I can claim?
The DApp reads the currently available reward from the staking contract and displays it in your dashboard. The amount can change as the blockchain state changes, so the value shown by the DApp should be treated as the current on-chain amount.
How can I claim my rewards?
When a reward becomes available, the corresponding claim button becomes active. Select the claim option, review the transaction in your wallet, and confirm it. Your wallet must have enough native coin to pay the network transaction fee.
Why do I need the network's native coin?
The network's native coin is used to pay blockchain transaction fees, also known as gas. You need it for actions that require a transaction, such as staking, approval, or claiming rewards.
Why must the token be approved?
The staking token uses the standard ERC-20 approval mechanism. Approval gives the staking contract permission to transfer the amount you authorize when you make a stake. The approval itself does not create a staking position.
Does this DApp need my seed phrase?
No. Never enter your seed phrase or private key into this website. Transactions should be reviewed and signed only through your own wallet.
What happens if I leave the page?
Leaving the website does not remove your staking position. Your position remains recorded on the blockchain. When you return, reconnect the same wallet on the supported network and the DApp can read your current staking data again.
Why is my staking data not showing?
Check that you are using the same wallet address that was used for staking and that the wallet is on the supported network. If the wallet and network are correct but data is still missing, wait for the DApp to refresh and verify the transaction status on the blockchain explorer.
Is staking risk-free?
No. Staking involves smart-contract, token, market, and transaction risks. The value of the token can change, and blockchain transactions may have risks or limitations. Only use funds you are prepared to risk.
