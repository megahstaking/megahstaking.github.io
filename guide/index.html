What Is Megah Staking?

Megah Staking is a blockchain-based staking platform where users can deposit supported tokens into a staking program and receive rewards according to the rules of the active staking pool.

Your staking position is recorded on-chain. This means the important information related to your position and rewards is maintained by the smart contract rather than by a conventional centralized database.

The staking dashboard is designed to make this process simple: connect your wallet, stake your tokens, monitor your position, and claim available rewards when they become available.

How Can You Earn Rewards?

The program can provide more than one type of reward.

Staking ROI

Your staking position can generate a periodic ROI according to the reward rate configured for the staking program.

The active rate is determined by the staking contract and may change if the contract parameters are updated.

Sponsor Rewards

Users may also receive sponsor or matching rewards when qualifying activity occurs within their referral structure.

The amount and qualification depend on the reward rules configured for the staking program.

What Is ROI in Staking?

ROI, or Return on Investment, represents the reward generated relative to your staking position.

In this staking program, the reward calculation is associated with your active staking amount and the configured reward rate.

The exact amount available to claim is calculated from the current on-chain staking data. Therefore, the number displayed by the DApp can change as time passes and as your staking position changes.

What Is the Sponsor Reward?

The sponsor reward is a separate reward mechanism from your regular staking ROI.

When eligible activity generates a matching reward, the qualifying sponsor can accumulate that reward in their staking account.

Sponsor rewards are not the same as the ROI generated by your own staking position. They are tracked separately by the staking program.

Understanding the Maximum Cap

The maximum cap determines the reward limit associated with a staking position.

Your staking position has a reward limit

A staking position is not designed to generate unlimited rewards indefinitely. The smart contract maintains a maximum reward cap for each staking position.

As eligible rewards are accumulated and claimed, they count toward the applicable limit defined by the staking program.

Once the applicable maximum is reached, additional ROI rewards for that position are no longer accumulated beyond that limit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to common questions before you start staking.

Is this actually staking?
Yes. You deposit the supported token into the staking program and your staking position is recorded on-chain. The DApp uses the term staking because the platform operates through a deployed smart contract that maintains the staking positions and reward rules.
Do I receive rewards automatically?
Eligible rewards are calculated by the staking program according to its active rules. The amount shown as available to claim depends on your individual staking position and the current on-chain state.
Is the reward rate fixed forever?
Not necessarily. The staking contract contains configurable parameters. The DApp reads the active parameters from the contract rather than relying on a fixed value displayed only by the website.
What is ROI?
ROI means Return on Investment. In this program, it refers to the reward generated by your staking position according to the active reward rules.
What is the sponsor reward?
Sponsor rewards are a separate reward mechanism associated with qualifying activity in the referral structure. They are tracked separately from the ROI generated by your own stake.
What is the Maximum Cap?
The Maximum Cap is the maximum reward limit associated with your staking position. It prevents a position from accumulating unlimited ROI beyond the limit defined by the staking program.
Can I see my staking information on-chain?
Yes. The staking position and relevant reward data are maintained by the deployed smart contract. The DApp reads this information to display your current staking status.
Do I need to keep my wallet connected?
You need a connected wallet when you want to perform actions that require transaction authorization, such as staking or claiming rewards. You can still visit the website without signing a transaction.
Why do I need network gas?
Blockchain transactions require the native gas currency of the network. You should keep enough native currency in your wallet to pay transaction fees.
Does the website need my seed phrase?
No. Never enter your seed phrase, private key or wallet recovery phrase into the staking website. Transactions should be authorized directly through your wallet.
What happens if I close the website?
Closing the website does not erase your on-chain staking position. When you return, the DApp can read the staking information associated with your wallet from the blockchain.
Is staking risk-free?
No. Blockchain-based staking involves risks, including smart-contract risk, token price volatility, network risk and changes to the economic conditions of the program. Only stake an amount you understand and can afford to risk.
Important before you participate

Staking rewards are not guaranteed profits and the future market value of the token can change significantly. Always verify the official website, token contract, staking contract, network and transaction details before signing a transaction. Never share your private key or seed phrase.
