1. Introduction

MEGAH Staking is a decentralized Web3 application ("dApp") that allows users to stake $MEGAH tokens on the BNB Smart Chain through a transparent, non-custodial smart contract.

These Terms explain how the platform functions, what you can expect, and the risks involved. By connecting your wallet and interacting with the smart contract, you agree to these Terms.

⚡ Key Point: This is a non-custodial platform. Your tokens are always in your wallet until you stake them into the smart contract. We never hold, store, or control your funds.

2. System Mechanics

The MEGAH Staking system operates through a verified smart contract deployed on BNB Smart Chain. All interactions are on-chain and publicly verifiable.

🟢 Daily ROI Read from contract (dynamic, admin-updatable)
🟣 10-Level Referral Bonus Percentages read directly from referralBonusLevels array
🟡 Leader Bonus Extra bonus for registered Leaders (not included in Max Cap)
🔴 Maximum Cap 200% of your total staked amount — protects system sustainability

📊 How ROI is calculated:
Daily ROI = (Staked Amount × DailyROI_BPS) / 10000
Example: Stake 1,000 MEGAH → Earn ROI per day based on current dailyRoiBps

🔗 Referral System:
When your downline claims ROI, you receive referral bonuses from all 10 levels automatically. You do not need to be a Leader to earn referral bonuses — referral bonuses are available to everyone with active staking who hasn't reached Max Cap. Percentages are read directly from the contract's referralBonusLevels.

3. Staking Rules

⚠️ IMPORTANT
Staked tokens are PERMANENTLY LOCKED. Once you stake $MEGAH into the smart contract, you cannot withdraw the principal amount. Only rewards (ROI, referral bonuses, leader bonuses) can be claimed.

Before you stake, ensure you understand:

  • Minimum Stake: Read from contract (minStake) — subject to admin updates
  • Referrer: Optional on first stake — once set via userReferrers, it cannot be changed
  • Gas Fees: All transactions require BNB for gas on BNB Smart Chain
  • Top-up: Adding more stake saves your pending ROI (savedReward) and resets your last claim time

💡 Tip: Use the "Estimator" tab to calculate potential earnings before committing your tokens.

🎯 Pro Tip: The more active downlines you have, the faster you reach your Max Cap. Build your referral network and claim regularly!

5. Referral & Leader System

📢 FOR EVERYONE
You do NOT need to be a Leader to earn referral bonuses.
Anyone with active staking and not at Max Cap automatically receives 10-Level Referral Bonuses from their referral network via pendingReferralBonus.

How the 10-Level Referral System works:

  • 📤 When your downline claims ROI, bonuses are distributed to all eligible uplines
  • 💰 Each level receives a percentage from referralBonusLevels[i]
  • 🔄 Eligibility: active stake + not reached Max Cap (totalEarned < maxCap)
  • 🔍 The contract traverses up to 10 levels via userReferrers mapping
  • 📤 Undistributed bonuses go to ADMIN_LEFTOVER_WALLET

Leader System (Extra Bonus):

  • 👑 Leaders are registered by the admin via setLeader() — not automatic
  • 📤 Leader Bonus (from leaderBonusBps) is on top of referral bonuses
  • 💎 Leader Bonus is NOT counted toward Max Cap
  • 🔍 Contract searches for the nearest Leader via _findLeader() (up to 10 levels)

📊 Example:
Your downline claims 100 MEGAH ROI:
→ You (regular user) earn: Level 1 referral bonus from referralBonusLevels[0]
→ If you're also a Leader: Referral Bonus + Leader Bonus from leaderBonusBps 🎉

6. Maximum Cap (200%)

🛡️ What is Max Cap?
Max Cap = Total Staked × 2
Example: Stake 100 MEGAH → Max Cap = 200 MEGAH
Stored in userStakes[user].maxCap

How Max Cap works:

  • ✅ Total rewards (ROI + Referral Bonus) cannot exceed 200% of your staked amount
  • ✅ Once totalEarned >= maxCap, you stop earning ROI and referral bonuses
  • 👑 Leader Bonus continues — it's EXEMPT from Max Cap
  • 📊 Track your progress in the Dashboard progress bar
  • 🔍 Check via isMaxCapReached(address)

⚠️ Important:
Max Cap protects the system from inflation. It's a feature, not a bug. Plan your staking strategy accordingly.
